The Programme Exposure Test

Your programme runs on assumptions. Some of them are ageing.

Every programme is built on assumptions — about who it reaches, which institutions it depends on, and which conditions will hold long enough for the design to work. Policy, administration, funding, adjacent systems and evidence keep moving around those assumptions, often outside the programme team's normal monitoring.

This diagnostic doesn't ask whether your programme is being monitored. It asks whether your team could tell if the ground underneath it had shifted — and where, specifically, that visibility is thinnest.
